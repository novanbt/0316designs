About 0316 Designs

Spaces That Reflect The Way You Live.

0316 Designs is a boutique interior design practice based in Singapore. We specialize in residential renovations, bespoke carpentry, spatial planning, and high-precision execution for discerning homeowners.

Curated by Shawn Tan with over a decade of experience, we view each project as a site-specific dialogue between light, material texture, and human ritual. Our interiors balance clean minimalist lines with warm, natural materiality.
